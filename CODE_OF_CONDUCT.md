# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in Browser-Agent a respectful and constructive experience
for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and implementations rather than people.
- Accept good-faith disagreement and explain technical concerns with evidence.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's secrets, browsing data, or private
  communications.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, personal attacks, discrimination, or deliberate intimidation.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or knowingly misleading contributors.
- Using project channels to promote unsafe behavior or bypass project security controls.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through the private contact route
listed on the repository profile. Include relevant context and links, but do not expose private data
publicly. The maintainer will review reports fairly and may remove content, reject contributions, or
restrict participation where necessary.

This project adopts the Contributor Covenant in spirit; before public launch, the maintainer should
verify the exact text/version and decide whether to replace this file with the complete official
version.
